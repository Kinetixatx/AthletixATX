Understanding Your DEXA Scan

Know what your body is made of. Build a plan that works.

What Is a DEXA Scan?

A DEXA scan estimates your body fat, lean tissue, and bone mineral. It reveals information a bathroom scale cannot.

Body Fat Percentage

Body fat percentage estimates the proportion of your weight that comes from fat. Interpret it alongside your strength, health history, and goals.

Lean Mass

Lean mass includes muscle and other non-fat, non-bone tissue. Resistance training and adequate nutrition help preserve muscle and strength.

Visceral Fat: Why It Matters

Visceral fat surrounds organs deep inside your abdomen. Elevated levels are associated with insulin resistance, type 2 diabetes, and cardiovascular disease—even at a normal body weight.

A visceral fat estimate is a risk indicator, not a diagnosis. Exercise, nutrition, sleep, and appropriate medical care can help address your overall risk.

Bone Health

Bone mineral measurements provide useful context, but diagnosing osteoporosis requires appropriate clinical testing and interpretation.
